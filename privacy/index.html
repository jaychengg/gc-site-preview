Privacy Policy

Working draft. This text describes the intended handling of enquiry data and is pending legal review before the website is published.

Who We Are

This website is operated by Gold Classic Enterprise Ltd., 15F., No. 866-1, Jhongjheng Rd., Jhonghe Dist., New Taipei City 235, Taiwan (“Gold Classic”, “we”). For any privacy question or request, write to info@gold-classic.com.

Information You Give Us

When you send an enquiry through the contact form, we ask for:

  • your name, company, business email address and country or region;
  • what you are interested in, the product category and the product or application;
  • an estimated annual volume, if you choose to give one;
  • your message, and any drawings or reference files you choose to attach.

Please do not include personal information about other people, or confidential information you are not entitled to share.

How We Use It

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotations, assess technical feasibility and maintain a record of our business correspondence with you. We do not sell personal information and we do not use enquiry data for unrelated marketing.

Who Receives It

Enquiries are handled by the Gold Classic team in Taiwan. Where a technical assessment is needed, relevant details and drawings are shared with our factory in Vietnam. Service providers that host this website or deliver email process data on our behalf. Your information may therefore be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including Taiwan and Vietnam.

How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any business relationship that follows, and afterwards only as long as required for legal or accounting purposes. You may ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Cookies and Analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If we introduce usage analytics, we will choose a tool that avoids unnecessary collection of personal data and will update this policy before doing so.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address above. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to This Policy

We will publish any changes on this page.
