Manufacturing/Precision CNC

Precision CNC. From Drawing to Repeatable Detail.

In brief

Critical dimensions are machined within one coordinated production system, connecting engineering, material, finishing and inspection from the start.

On a glass fitting, a few features do most of the work: the hole positions that meet the glass, the faces that seat a gasket, the bore a pin turns in. We identify those features on the drawing, machine them from forged, cast or bar material, and check them in the same way at the first piece and through the batch. The aim is parts that assemble and move the same way each time, within the tolerances agreed for that part.

One Part, from Drawing to Batch.

  1. 01

    Drawing Review

    Critical features and their tolerances are identified with the customer before anything is cut.

  2. 02

    Machining Set-Up

    Fixtures, tooling and programme are prepared around the features that matter most.

  3. 03

    First-Piece Confirmation

    The first part is measured against the drawing before the batch is released.

  4. 04

    Batch Production

    In-process checks follow the same features through the production run.

  5. 05

    Final Inspection

    Parts are inspected against the drawing and the agreed inspection plan before finishing or packing.

Machined with the Finish in Mind.

Polishing removes material and plating adds it. A dimension that matters on the finished product has to be planned before the first cut, not corrected after the last one.

Because machining, polishing and plating belong to the same production system, the allowance for each is set by people who see the part at every stage — and a fit that changes after finishing is traced back to its cause in the same building.

Technical notes

Details and Limits.

Specific values are confirmed per product in the quotation or on the approved drawing. We would rather state a limit than imply a promise.

Materials machined
Brass and stainless steel, from bar stock, hot forgings and investment castings.
Tolerances
Agreed per part. Achievable tolerance depends on material, geometry, feature size and the measurement method, so we do not quote a single figure for all products — and we do not claim zero deviation.
Inspection
Dimensional checks against the drawing at first piece and during production. Project-specific inspection plans can be discussed at quotation stage.
What to send
A 2D drawing with tolerances, a 3D model if available, the material and finish required, and the expected annual quantity.
