03From concept to completion

From First Drawing to Packed Carton.

See the full process

  1. 01

    Design

    We review the application, drawing, material and function with you.

  2. 02

    Prototyping

    Samples check structure, assembly and surface before tooling is committed.

  3. 03

    Tooling

    Moulds and dies are built and adjusted in our own tooling facility.

  4. 04

    Production

    Investment casting or hot forging, followed by CNC machining of critical features.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    Polishing and in-house plating to the approved surface and colour.

  6. 06

    Quality Control

    Parts are checked against the drawing, the approved sample and the inspection plan.

  7. 07

    Packaging

    Packed for your brand, channel and shipping requirements.
