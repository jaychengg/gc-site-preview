Weather Is Part of the Design.

In brief

Products for bathrooms and exposed outdoor settings are developed as material-and-finish systems, with the surrounding environment considered from the start.

Steam, standing water, cleaning agents, temperature swings and airborne deposits all act on metal — and they act differently on a shower hinge than on a balcony bracket. Durability in these places does not come from a coating added at the end. It comes from choosing the base material, the finish, the construction details and the care routine together, and from being clear about where a given combination should not be used.

Three Environments, Three Different Briefs.

A

Bathrooms and Wet Areas

  • Daily wetting and drying, warm humid air and condensation.
  • Soap residue and household cleaners, some of them aggressive to metal finishes.
  • Moving parts — hinges and sliding hardware — that must keep working while wet.

B

Exposed Outdoor Applications

  • Rain, temperature change and sunlight over long periods.
  • Airborne dirt and deposits that settle on surfaces and hold moisture.
  • Places where water can collect: joints, fixings and horizontal faces.

C

Aggressive Environments

  • Coastal air, swimming-pool atmospheres and chemical exposure are separate cases.
  • Suitability is assessed for the specific product, material and finish. It is never implied by general outdoor use.
  • Tell us about these conditions before selection, not after installation.

Not every Gold Classic product is suitable for coastal, pool or chemical environments.

Four Decisions, Made Together.

When you describe the environment, we review four things as one system instead of four separate specifications.

  • Base material — brass or stainless steel, and which alloy or grade.
  • Finish — a natural metal surface or a plated finish, and how it is prepared.
  • Construction detail — where water can sit, how parts drain, and which metals and gaskets touch.
  • Care — what cleaning the finish needs, and which products it must be kept away from.

Tested as Components, Not as Claims.

Hardware is checked in our quality laboratory before it leaves the factory. Test results belong to the product, material and finish they were obtained on, and we share them on that basis.

Technical notes

Details and Limits.

Specific values are confirmed per product in the quotation or on the approved drawing. We would rather state a limit than imply a promise.

Test data
Test methods and results are provided for the specific product, material and finish on which they were obtained. We do not publish a single corrosion rating for the whole range.
Outside general statements
Coastal and marine locations, indoor and outdoor pools, and chemical or industrial atmospheres. Suitability for these is confirmed individually, in writing, for a named product and finish.
Maintenance
Regular cleaning is part of durability. Deposits left on a surface are a common cause of staining, including on stainless steel outdoors. Use mild, non-abrasive cleaners and rinse with clean water.
What to tell us
Indoor, bathroom or outdoor; distance from the sea; pool or chemical exposure; cleaning regime; and the service life you are designing for.
