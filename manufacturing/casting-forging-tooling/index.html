Manufacturing/Casting, Forging & Tooling

Built Around Your Product.

In brief

Our in-house tooling facility connects engineering directly with casting and forging, allowing designs to be developed, tested and refined with fewer handovers on the way to production.

When the people who design a die work beside the people who run the press, a trial result becomes a tooling adjustment without a purchase order in between. That shortens the loop between what a customer asked for and what comes off the tool. The result is a product that reaches production-ready form more accurately and more quickly, with the reason for every change understood by one team.

From Requirement to Approved Production.

The tooling facility is the hinge of this sequence: steps three to five repeat inside our own factory until the part is right.

  1. 01

    Requirement Review

    Drawing, sample or application brief — we establish what the part must do and how it will be finished.

  2. 02

    Engineering Assessment

    Process route, parting lines, draft and machining allowance are worked out before tooling begins.

  3. 03

    Tool Design and Build

    Moulds and dies are designed and made in our own tooling department.

  4. 04

    Trial Production

    First parts are cast or forged on the production equipment they will run on.

  5. 05

    Measure and Adjust

    Trial parts are measured; the tool is corrected in-house and trialled again.

  6. 06

    Production Approval

    Approved samples and dimensions become the reference for repeat production.

Investment Casting or Hot Forging?

Both are available in our own factory, so the recommendation follows the part — not the limits of a single process.

A

Investment Casting

When the shape is the challenge.

  • Complex external forms, internal features and undercuts that would be costly to machine.
  • Formed close to final shape, so machining concentrates on the critical features.
  • Tooling: moulds for wax patterns.

B

Hot Forging

When the structure and surface are the challenge.

  • A dense, sound structure — a good foundation for polishing and plating.
  • Well suited to brass hinge bodies and similar solid components produced in repeat batches.
  • Tooling: forging dies, made and adjusted in our own tooling department.

The choice depends on geometry, strength, surface requirement, quantity and material. We recommend a route during drawing review.

Part size and weight
Assessed against the press and die for each component during drawing review.
Geometry
Solid, load-bearing forms with machined interfaces: hole positions, mating faces and bores are finished by CNC after forging.
Batch size
Repeat production with dedicated dies. Minimum and typical batch sizes are agreed per component.
After forging
Trimming, CNC machining, polishing, in-house plating, inspection and packing within the same production system.
