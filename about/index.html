About Gold Classic

Three Generations. More than 50 Years of Hardware and Manufacturing Experience.

01 — Family experience

Gold Classic is a family-led precision manufacturer with more than 50 years of experience across three generations.

Our experience spans three generations and more than five decades in hardware and precision manufacturing. That continuity shapes how we evaluate designs, manage production and support long-term customer relationships.

It also explains what we choose to make. We specialise in precision stainless-steel and brass products, where material selection determines how a product machines, finishes and performs over time — and where judgement built over decades is difficult to replace with equipment alone.

02 — Gold Classic

One team for the customer, from the first conversation to delivery.

Gold Classic is the company customers speak to, contract with and hold responsible. From Taiwan, our team handles enquiries, product development, international business and project follow-up.

Gold Classic manufactures high-end stainless-steel and brass products for professional hardware and architectural applications, and manages the complete process from design and prototyping to finished, inspected and packaged products.

03 — Our factory

Our factory in Vietnam brings the whole process into one production system.

In Thai Binh, Vietnam, our factory combines casting, forging, CNC machining, polishing, plating, assembly and quality control within one coordinated production system. Tooling is designed and made in-house, and plating runs on our own line.
