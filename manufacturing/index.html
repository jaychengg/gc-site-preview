Manufacturing

Our Factory in Vietnam.

Casting, forging, CNC machining, polishing, plating, assembly and quality control work within one coordinated production system — with tooling made in-house.

Follow the process

The Site

Thai Binh, Vietnam. Production halls, tooling, plating lines, a test laboratory and raw-material warehousing.

One Team. One Production System.

Customers work with one Gold Classic team from the initial concept through production and delivery, supported by our factory in Vietnam. Requirements, drawings and approvals are handled by Gold Classic; the factory turns them into tooling, parts, finishes and packed goods.

The factory operates under the name VTAC. It is where every process described on this page takes place.

01End-to-end process

From the Drawing to the Carton.

Nine stages, one organisation. What matters is not the list of steps but that each is managed with knowledge of the one before and the one after.

  1. 01

    Design Support

    Understanding the application, drawings, material and functional requirements.

  2. 02

    Prototyping

    Checking structure, assembly and surface effect early, while changes are still inexpensive.

  3. 03

    Tooling

    Our in-house tooling facility turns the requirement into moulds, dies and process conditions that can be adjusted and repeated.

  4. 04

    Casting & Forging

    Investment casting or hot forging, selected according to geometry, strength, quantity and material.

  5. 05

    CNC Machining

    Dimensions, hole positions and mating faces are machined where the drawing demands control.

  6. 06

    Polishing

    Surface flatness, grain and appearance are prepared for the intended finish.

  7. 07

    Plating & Finishing

    Electroplating and ED coating on our own lines, to the approved colour and surface.

  8. 08

    Quality Control

    Inspection against drawings, approved samples and the agreed inspection specification.

  9. 09

    Assembly & Packaging

    Assembled and packed according to brand, channel and transport requirements.

03Forging capability

Capacity Measured in Finished Products.

Our forging line supports repeatable production of architectural hardware components, from heated billet and precision tooling through trimming, CNC machining, polishing, plating and final inspection.

What We Make

  • Brass hinge bodies and hinge plates
  • Customer-specific brass components developed with in-house forging dies

Tell us the component you have in mind and we will confirm whether forging is the right route.

Process Envelope

Material
Hot-forged brass. The alloy is confirmed per product with the quotation.
Part size and weight
Assessed against the press and die for each component during drawing review.
Geometry
Solid, load-bearing forms with machined interfaces: hole positions, mating faces and bores are finished by CNC after forging.
Batch size
Repeat production with dedicated dies. Minimum and typical batch sizes are agreed per component.
After forging
Trimming, CNC machining, polishing, in-house plating, inspection and packing within the same production system.

How We State Capacity

The simplest measure is the one a project team can picture: bathrooms. For a specific component we then confirm output in pieces, once the geometry, alloy, finish and inspection requirements are known.
