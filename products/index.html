Different Ways of Working with Glass.

From a single fitting to a coordinated product range, Gold Classic supports distributors, glass professionals and hardware brands with products for interior, bathroom and exterior applications. Material, finish and product configuration are selected according to the intended use and project requirements.

01 / 05

Door & Shower Hardware

Fittings for glass doors, shower enclosures and related installations.

The range includes hinges, flush fittings, pull handles, knobs, gate hinges and latches in stainless-steel and brass options. Products can be supplied in selected finishes for coordinated bathroom, interior and entrance applications.

For product selection, confirm the glass configuration, glass thickness, door dimensions, opening direction, mounting condition and required finish. Load capacity and other performance values are stated only for the applicable product and installation.

Scale · We Supply

15,000 hotel bathrooms per month.
Fit out fifty 300-room hotels.

Railing & Balustrade Hardware

Components for glass railings, stairs, balconies, galleries and related architectural applications.

Gold Classic supplies brackets, adapters, posts, flanges, saddles, end caps, wall brackets and glass clamps for a range of railing layouts. Components are available for different connection details, profiles and finish requirements.

Indoor, bathroom and outdoor installations place different demands on the material and finish. Product selection must also consider the glass specification, supporting structure, fixing method, loads and applicable project requirements.

Final selection depends on project engineering, the glass specification, fixing conditions and load requirements, and must be confirmed by the qualified professionals responsible for the project.

Scale · We Supply

8 km of glass balustrade per month.
Line 3 Golden Gate Bridges.

03 / 05

Structural Glass Hardware

Precision fittings for point-supported and other architectural glass connections.

The Gold Classic range includes spider fittings and related glass connection components. Each fitting is manufactured with attention to geometry, hole positions, contact surfaces and finish quality so that the metal component can integrate cleanly with the glass and supporting structure.

Selection and use must follow the project design, glass type and thickness, fixing conditions, loads and applicable engineering requirements. Project-specific structural approval remains the responsibility of the relevant qualified professionals.

Final selection depends on project engineering, the glass specification, fixing conditions and load requirements. Gold Classic supplies components; it does not provide project structural approval.

Scale · We Supply

10,000+ glass connection points per month.
Glaze a football pitch of façade.

Sliding Components

Guides and related hardware for sliding glass applications.

Gold Classic supplies sliding guides and related components for customer and system-specific applications. Products are developed around the required glass configuration, mounting arrangement, movement and finish.

If the component forms part of an existing sliding system, provide the relevant drawing, sample or system reference so that dimensions, interfaces and manufacturing feasibility can be reviewed accurately.

OEM & Custom Components

Stainless-steel and brass components developed around your product requirements.

Gold Classic supports OEM, private-label and custom hardware projects from initial review through finished packaging. Customers may begin with a drawing, sample, existing product or application requirement.

Our engineering team works directly with our factory in Vietnam. Design review, prototyping, in-house tooling, CNC machining, investment casting or hot forging, polishing, plating, inspection and packaging can be managed within one coordinated manufacturing process.
